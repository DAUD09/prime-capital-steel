About Us

The story, mission and people behind Prime Capital Steel Fabricators.

Our Story

Built on Craftsmanship, Driven by Quality

Prime Capital Steel Fabricators was founded with a single purpose — to bring world-class steel fabrication to Malawi. What started as a small workshop in Lilongwe has grown into one of the country's most trusted names in custom steelwork, serving residential, commercial and industrial clients across the nation.

Every gate, staircase, carport and structure we produce is built with the same attention to detail and commitment to durability that has defined our reputation since day one.

  • Custom designs from concept to installation
  • Skilled, experienced and safety-conscious team
  • Premium grade steel and materials throughout
  • On-time delivery and transparent pricing
